# Madhav E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, profit, product categories, customer purchasing patterns, and payment preferences.

The objective is to understand business performance through interactive visualizations and identify trends that can support better business decision-making.

![Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Quantity:** 6K
- **Total Sales Amount:** 438K
- **Total Profit:** 37K
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** 121K

## Key Features & Analysis

- **Sales Performance:** Analysis of total sales across different states.
- **Profit Trends:** Monthly profit analysis to identify profitable and loss-making periods.
- **Category Analysis:** Distribution of product quantities across Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture.
- **Customer Analysis:** Identification of top customers based on sales contribution.
- **Payment Mode Analysis:** Breakdown of transactions across COD, UPI, debit cards, and credit cards.
- **Sub-Category Analysis:** Comparison of profit across different product sub-categories.

## Key Insights

- Clothing contributes the largest share of total quantity sold.
- Printers and Bookcases are among the leading sub-categories by profit.
- COD accounts for the largest share of orders by payment quantity.
- Sales contributions vary across states, with Uttar Pradesh leading in the displayed analysis.
- Monthly profit trends show fluctuations, with some months recording negative profits.
